The Misunderstood Seers Are Coming Out

The Lord Jesus Christ is the Father, our Father in Heaven, and the Holy Spirit are raising up the seers.

Long misunderstood. Long misjudged. Long hidden. We’re coming out — little by little.

And the one thing the body could do is to not attack its own body — especially if they don’t understand that particular part, or its use.

I would urge you: before you ever say that a seer is not seeing correctly, or that didn’t happen, or that’s not done anymore — I would urge you to pray. Ask God to expose the person to you. Ask Him to show you who the person is in the realm of the Spirit.

“Have nothing to do with the fruitless deeds of darkness,
but rather expose them.”

Ephesians 5 : 11

Do not partake in the evil works of darkness. Instead, expose them. And stop seeking exposure.

The only One who knows our heart in its entirety has the ability to expose — and that is our Father in Heaven. The only one besides Abba Father who seeks to expose is Satan, the accuser.

So before you seek to expose a person, be sure that you are not making Satan’s job easier for him.

The misunderstood seers are being raised up and trained. And we’re going to be exposing in the future.

More about Jesus. And nothing about darkness — because the darkness is already defeated by the King of Kings and the Lord of Lords.

The end. Until next time,
Tenisha
